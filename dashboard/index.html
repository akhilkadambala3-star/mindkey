    How MindKey works

    • 1 Learns your baseline. MindKey watches how you usually type — speed, timing, rhythm — and builds a personal reference from your own sessions.
    • 2 Notices change over time. It looks for patterns that persist across many sessions, not single unusual moments.
    • 3 Combines signals. Typing behavior, how you say you're feeling, and any symptoms you report are considered together before anything is recommended.

    MindKey is a screening tool that looks for persistent changes in your own typing behavior over time. It does not diagnose dementia, Parkinson's disease, or any other condition.

    Wellbeing check-in

    How are you feeling today?

    A quick answer helps MindKey understand whether anything everyday — like a late night or a stressful day — could explain a recent variation. There are no wrong answers.

    Why check in?

    Typing can vary for many reasons. When a variation lines up with something you've told us about — stress, poor sleep, feeling unwell — MindKey treats it as contextualized: it stays in your history, but it's understood differently.

    A variation is only flagged as persistent when it remains unexplained across several sessions.

    Why are we recommending this?

      MindKey never presents a disease risk percentage. It compares you with your own baseline and combines multiple signals before suggesting a next step — and the next step is almost always simply talking to a professional.

      This insight is not a medical diagnosis. It is an observation about your typing behavior over time, intended to support — never replace — a conversation with a qualified healthcare professional.

      If you're concerned about changes in your health, the most direct next step is to contact your usual doctor. MindKey is here to help you start that conversation with more context — it does not replace medical advice.

      What MindKey collects

      • Typing timing and rhythm — dwell time, flight time, speed, pauses, corrections
      • When sessions happen and how long they last
      • Aggregated behavioral metrics built from those sessions
      • Check-in responses, if you choose to give them
      • Symptom responses, only if you complete the optional questionnaire

      What MindKey never needs

      • The content of what you type — messages, documents, or code
      • Passwords or anything entered into a password field
      • Private conversation text in any app
      • The words themselves — only the timing between key presses matters

      Where processing happens

      The desktop agent extracts typing features locally on your device and only timing-derived aggregates are sent to the server. The words you type never leave your computer. Planned: run more of the analysis — including anomaly detection — entirely on-device.
